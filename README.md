# **Black Clover**
The story takes place in the Clover Kingdom, where magic is the foundation of society. It primarily follows two orphans who grew up together, Asta and Yuno, who both aspire to become the next Wizard King.

The main Characters are:
1. Asta: The loud, overly energetic protagonist who, against all odds, was born with absolutely no magic
2. Yuno: A quiet, stoic prodigy who was born with immense magical power and the talent to control Wind Magic.
